# ESA image-use permission record

**Reference:** ESA HQ PHOTOS 20260819-0333
**Date of grant:** 2026-08-19
**Parties:** repository maintainer (query of 2026-08-17) and ESA image distribution (spaceinimages@esa.int)
**Full correspondence:** held privately by the maintainer; cite the ESA reference above.

## What was asked

1. Whether cropped and background-removed derivatives of eleven ESA spacecraft renders could be produced for informational, non-commercial display.
2. Whether ESA controls reuse rights for images credited to contractors (ESA/ATG medialab, ESA–P. Carril, ESA/AOES Medialab, ESA/Mlabspace, ESA/Denmann production).
3. Whether permission could extend to future ESA-copyright imagery added to the collection, without image-by-image requests.

## Rulings

1. **Adaptations.** Minor adaptations — cropping, aspect-ratio changes, resizing — are permitted. **Background removal is not permitted** ("would fundamentally change" the image). ESA notes that official "clean" versions already exist for many missions.
2. **Contractor-credited images.** No further permissions are needed for the credits listed above.
3. **Future ESA images.** Granted under the ESA Standard Licence, with correct credit and licence information, subject to the standing condition below.

ESA classed this repository's use as non-commercial/informational.

## Standing condition

For each ESA image used, the repository and any site displaying it must state that the image is available **only under the ESA Standard Licence**, linking to:
https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Copyright_Notice_Images

This is recorded per file as `licenceNoticeUrl` in `index.json`.

## Consequences applied in this repository

- Background-removed derivatives of ESA Standard Licence images are permanently prohibited; the `tools/licenses.py` gate is the enforcement point.
- Display versions come from ESA's official clean renders, stored as `<folder>-photo-clean*` assets (resize-only; no other modification).
