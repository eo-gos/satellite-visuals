### Asset licensing policy

- Vector assets: All `.svg` files under `satellites/` and `other-spacecraft/` are original works created by the maintainers and are licensed under CC BY 4.0, the same as other original materials in this repository.
- Raster assets: The `*-512px.png` / `*-1024px.png` files are renders of this repository's own SVGs (regenerated with `tools/render_pngs.mjs`) and are therefore also CC BY 4.0. Photograph files (`*-photo.*`) and their cutouts (`*-photo-cut-*px.png`) are third-party works under their own licences — see the sourced-photographs and derived-forms bullets below. Earlier revisions of this repository contained rasters resized from third-party websites; those were replaced in July 2026 and are not licensed by this repository — any use of them must comply with the original rights holder's terms.
- Sourced photographs: real satellite photos/renders will be added only where the owner has granted an open licence (public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA, OGL, agency media terms permitting reuse), with the licence and credit recorded per image in `index.json` (`imageLicense`, `imageCredit`) before the file is committed. An operator's published press/media-page terms permitting use with credit also qualify — recorded as `imageStatus: media-terms` with the terms page as `imageSourceURL` (see the Tier D ladder in `TASKING.md`). Explicit written permission from the owner (e.g. an email reply) likewise qualifies — note sender and date in `ATTRIBUTIONS.csv`. **Attribution is not a licence** — recording where an image came from does not create permission to host it. Never source images from general web/image search.
- **ESA Standard Licence — accepted (decision, 2026-08-15).** ESA's [terms of use](https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Terms_and_conditions_of_use_of_images_and_videos_available_on_the_esa_website) permit educational, editorial and informational use with the credit `©ESA`, which is the "agency media terms permitting reuse" case above. Spacecraft renders on esa.int are almost never CC-licensed (ESA's Creative Commons subset is overwhelmingly its *data* imagery), so the Standard Licence is the only route to ESA hardware imagery. Recorded as `imageLicense: ESA Standard Licence`, `imageStatus: licensed`, with the esa.int image page as `imageSourceURL` and the credit line copied verbatim from that page. **Where a page offers CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO as an alternative, take the CC option** — it is the stronger grant. Two caveats: the terms exclude commercial use and do not grant redistribution explicitly, and most render pages add "additional permission may be required (contact spaceinimages@esa.int)"; both were reviewed and accepted. A verbatim snapshot of the terms text is kept at `docs/licence-snapshots/esa-standard-licence.md`. What may be *done* with these images was put to ESA (spaceinimages@esa.int) in August 2026 and answered in writing — see the next bullet.
- **ESA Standard Licence — usable, confirmed in writing (ESA HQ PHOTOS 20260819-0333, 2026-08-19).** ESA's image distribution team answered the August 2026 query and classed this repository's use as non-commercial/informational. The permission record is `docs/permissions/esa-20260819-0333.md`; cite the ESA reference in any question about scope. What it settles:
  - **Adaptations.** Cropping, aspect-ratio changes and resizing are permitted. **Background removal is not, ever** — ESA ruled it "would fundamentally change" the image. This is a permanent prohibition, not a pending question: `tools/licenses.py` keeps `permits_derivatives("ESA Standard Licence")` at `False`, and `derivatives_refused()` hard-blocks the cut pass for this licence — `tools/process_photos.py` refuses these photos even under `--allow-nonderiv`, because that override means "permission was confirmed", and here the confirmation was a refusal.
  - **Licence notice (standing condition).** Every ESA image used must carry its correct credit *and* a statement that the image is available only under the ESA Standard Licence, linking to <https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Copyright_Notice_Images>. The link is recorded per entry as `licenceNoticeUrl` in `index.json` — **`index.json` only**, no new `ATTRIBUTIONS.csv` column — and any site displaying the image must render it alongside the credit. `python3 tools/check_index.py` validates the invariant (every ESA entry carries exactly this URL, no non-ESA entry carries the field) — run it at PR review.
  - **Contractor credits cleared.** Images credited ESA/ATG medialab, ESA–P. Carril, ESA/AOES Medialab, ESA/Mlabspace and ESA/Denmann production need no further permission; ESA controls the reuse rights.
  - **Future ESA images.** Blanket permission for ESA-copyright imagery added later, under the same conditions (correct credit + licence notice) — no per-image asks.
- Clean display versions of ESA images: because background removal is prohibited, the portal's cut-out slot is filled from ESA's *own* official "clean" renders (plain or transparent background) published on the same multimedia library, sourced with the usual paperwork. Naming mirrors the cutouts: `<folder>-photo-clean.<ext>` is the archival original as ESA published it, and `<folder>-photo-clean-1024px.png` / `<folder>-photo-clean-512px.png` are the display versions. Those PNGs are **resize-only** — no matting, no background edits, no compositing, nothing that changes the image beyond scale. Sourcing them is curator work (issue #125); no clean assets are committed yet.
- The `imageSourceURL` / `imageRightsHolder` / `imageSourceTier` / `imageStatus` fields in `index.json` describe the *photo-sourcing lead* for each mission (where a licensed photo is expected to come from and the state of acquiring it), not the committed files. Committed files are all repo-original until an entry's `imageStatus` records a confirmed licence.
- `imageStatus` vocabulary (canonical set):
  - `licensed` — a photo is committed with its licence confirmed and paperwork complete.
  - `media-terms` — hosted under an operator's published media-page terms (credit as specified).
  - `trademark-editorial-use` — logos identified next to their own missions under brand-page terms (see Task C in `TASKING.md`).
  - `svg-fallback` — decided: no photo will be sourced; the house SVG is the visual.
  - `pending-public-domain` / `pending-cc-by-sa` / `pending-agency-terms` — a sourcing lead is identified and the licence class is known; acquisition not yet done.
  - `needs-permission-or-svg-fallback` — Tier D: waiting on the permission ladder; falls back to the SVG if permission never lands.
- Derived/processed forms: cutouts (`-photo-cut-*px.png`) are mechanical derivatives (crop + background removal) of the committed photo. **Our processing adds no new rights — the source licence flows through unchanged, with modifications indicated** in the file's own `ATTRIBUTIONS.csv` row (which also records the tool, model and licence deed URL). CC BY-SA cutouts remain CC BY-SA. Media-terms assets are not processed without checking the terms permit modification (`tools/process_photos.py` refuses by default). ESA Standard Licence photos are never cut at all — see the ESA bullets above. Manual mask touch-ups must be noted in the row.
- See `ATTRIBUTIONS.csv` for per-file provenance (source URL, rights holder, stated license/terms). Where an `.svg` is original, the rights holder can be listed as the repository maintainers and the license as CC BY 4.0.

If you are a rights holder and want an asset removed or corrected, please open an issue or contact the maintainer.
